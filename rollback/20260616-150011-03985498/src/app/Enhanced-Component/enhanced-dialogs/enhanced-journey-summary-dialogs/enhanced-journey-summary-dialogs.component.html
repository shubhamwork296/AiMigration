Why's that?

  • This message is displayed when a service has no reservation available.
  • The train might be fully booked if you’re searching at a short notice or if there’s a big event going on.
  • The train might be waiting for confirmation to run on the scheduled time if you’re planning ahead but tickets aren’t available.
Check out our timetable page

Can I still get on the same train?

  • Yes, you can still get on our unreserved coaches without a reservation.
  • If you're comfortable without a guaranteed seat, you can purchase a flexible Off-Peak or Anytime ticket online for your chosen travel date and board your preferred train. You can also purchase a ticket on your date of travel at the station.
  • For unreserved Standard Class seats, please head to:
    • Pendolino Trains: Coach C (Plus Coaches U & G on 11-car trains)
    • Evero Trains: Coach C (Plus Coach N on 10-car trains)

What if I prefer a guaranteed seat?

  • If you prefer a guaranteed seat, you can choose a different class of travel on this train or opt for a train at a different time.

Why's that?

If you're searching at quite short notice or if there's a big event going on, this train might be fully booked. If you're planning ahead and tickets aren't showing for a specific train, we might still be awaiting confirmation that the train will run at the times advertised. Check out our timetables page for more information.

Still want to go?

If this train is the one for you, you have a couple of options. We have unreserved coaches on all our trains so, if you don't mind not being guaranteed a seat, you can buy a flexible Off-Peak or Anytime ticket at the station without a reservation, then use your ticket's flexibility to hop on your preferred train. Head to coach C, U or G on our Pendolino trains, or coach C, H or N on Evero trains to find unreserved Standard Class seats.

If you'd rather be sure of a seat, try another class of travel on this train, or a train at another time.
