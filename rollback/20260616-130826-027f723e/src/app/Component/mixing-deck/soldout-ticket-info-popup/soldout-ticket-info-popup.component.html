Still want to go?

If this train is the one for you, you have a couple of options. We have unreserved coaches on all our trains so, if you don't mind not being guaranteed a seat, you can buy a flexible Off-Peak or Anytime ticket at the station without a reservation, then use your ticket's flexibility to hop on your preferred train. Head to coach C on our Pendolino trains (and also coach U on 11-coach trains) or coach C on Evero (and also coach J on 10-coach trains) to find unreserved Standard Class seats.

If you'd rather be sure of a seat, try either another class of travel on this train, or a train at another time.
