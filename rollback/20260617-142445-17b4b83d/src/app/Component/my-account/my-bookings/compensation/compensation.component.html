Compensation

Refund applications

You can apply for a refund if you do not end up travelling on the train you have booked, either because your plans changed, or because the train was cancelled or severely delayed. You must apply within 28 days of the date you were due to have travelled on.

If you would like to get a refund or amend your booking to a different date or time because your plans have changed, you will be charged a £10 admin fee per ticket. Not all ticket types are refundable. To find out more click here

Compensation claims

If you hold a valid single, return, or weekly ticket, you will be able to claim compensation for delays of 15 minutes or more, whatever the cause. To claim compensation and find out more click here

Automatic Delay Repay

If you buy a ticket and travel using Smartcard, we will automatically give you a refund whenever your journey is delayed for longer than two minutes. For example, if your journey is delayed for 14 minutes, we will automatically refund you 39p worth of credit into your c2c account. Your credit is available for you to redeem against future online purchases - helping you to save money. For more details, click here
